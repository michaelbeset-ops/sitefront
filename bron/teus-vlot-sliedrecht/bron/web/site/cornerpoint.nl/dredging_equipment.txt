 Dredging Equipment
 Are you looking for dredging equipment? Cornerpoint is your one-stop-shop for all new and used dredging equipment. We arrange the purchase and sale of dredge pumps, pump housings, wear plates and so on. Moreover, our excellent logistic service ensures your dredging equipment to arrive at any destination in the world as fast as possible. Service and expertise are after all our core values.
