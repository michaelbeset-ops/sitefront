 Cookie Policy 
 Cornerpoint B.V. makes use of cookies on this website. These are small files that are sent with pages of this website and stored to the hard drive of your computer by your browser. The information stored therein may be returned to our servers during a subsequent visit. This makes it easier for you to use the website. Cookies ensure, for example, that you can remain logged in to a website or that your preferences, such as location and language settings, are remembered.
 Types of cookies 
 Functional cookies
 These are cookies that are necessary for a website to function properly or that make it easier for you to use the website. Consider, for example, saving your login data so that you do not have to log in every time. Functional cookies also include storing the contents of your shopping cart or closing the cookie notification.
 Analytical cookies
 These are cookies that are placed to measure the visitor's behaviour on this website, in order to analyse it. We use this data to optimise the website.
 Tracking cookies
 These are cookies that are used to store your browsing behaviour within one or more websites. These can be used to create personal offers for you. The tracking cookies on this website are so-called 'third party cookies'. This means that these cookies are not placed by us, but by another party. This happens when you access files stored with the other party via this site. YouTube, for example. When you watch YouTube movies via this site, YouTube places a cookie.
 Consent 
 Not all cookies are designed to collect your data. Functional cookies , for example, do not collect any personal data. No consent is required for placing these cookies. They are immediately loaded when you visit the website, therefore.
 In the case of analytical cookies , it is sometimes necessary, but sometimes not, to request prior consent for placing these cookies. This depends on the settings used. This website uses Google Analytics. When configuring these, we have followed the instructions of the Dutch Data Protection Authority. Therefore, the settings are considered 'safe', so that this cookie may be placed without your consent. It is immediately loaded when you visit the website, therefore.
 Tracking cookies always require your consent before being placed. Therefore, these cookies are only loaded after you have given your permission to do so.
 Right to access and correction or erasure of data 
 You have the right to request access to and correction or erasure of your (personal) data. To prevent abuse we may request you to identify yourself adequately. When it concerns access to personal data linked to a cookie, you must send a copy of the relevant cookie. You can find this in your browser settings.
 Changes and contact 
 We may need to amend this cookie policy from time to time. We recommend you return here regularly to check if there are any changes. If you have any questions and/ or comments, please contact [email&#160;protected] .
