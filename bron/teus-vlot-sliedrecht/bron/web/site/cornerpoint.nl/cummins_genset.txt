 The function of a marine generator 
 A marine generator set is a combination of an engine and an alternator, and is used to provide energy. A genset can be the prime source of energy, used as a back-up where an outage can cause significant issues, or provide a supplementary power source when there's a rise in demand. A marine generator set can support numerous applications and is built for longevity.

 Selecting the right (Cummins) generator set 
 We understand that buying a (Cummins) generator is a major investment, therefore we are pleased to help you in selecting the right generator for your ship or vessel. We recommend that you pay attention to the following two points: 
 Selecting a generator that is too small, can lead to it wearing out quickly and requiring a lot of maintenance.  
 Selecting a generator that is too large will lead to too much carbon buildup and leaves unburned fuel in the exhaust. This will unevitably also lead to unnecessary repairs.
