 Trading diesel engine parts is our pleasure
 Cornerpoint has been internationally known for delivering diesel engine, ship engine parts and other components for maritime and/or industrial applications, including:
 • Engines
 • Couplings
 • Turbochargers
 • Camshafts
 • Cylinder heads
 • Connecting rods
 • Fuel injection lines or nozzles
 • Oil coolers
 • CCP parts
 • Separators
 • Sterntube seals
 • Reductions gearbox parts
 • All kind of pumps (e.g. plunger pumps) and pistons
 • Valves & Manifolds
 • Gasket sets
 • Refrigeration equipment
 • Ship gearbox 
 • Electrical parts
 • Dredging equipment
 • Processing equipment (fishing industry) etc.
 As you can see, no matter what you are looking for, Cornerpoint can find it for you. If your desired engine or ship engine part is not in stock , please send in a request and we will find you the missing component.
 Marine diesel engines and parts
 We repair and sell complete marine diesel engines and also ship engine parts from many different brands, including Caterpillar, Cummins Scania, Daf, Volvo, Daewoo and many others. These include new engines as well as budget-friendly options, i.e., reconditioned and/or overhauled engines. As a marine diesel specialist, we have been around for a long time (since the 90s), thus we can offer you the best available quality, against favourable prices. Contact us, we are happy to advise you on the correct engine or ship engine part for your ship or machine!
 Repair and complete overhaul of ship engines
 From a cylinder head overhaul to a complete reconditioning of your ship engine or replacing a ship engine part, we serve our customers worldwide with our excellent technical expertise.
 We ship engine parts worldwide
 We ship engine parts and marine accessories from The Netherlands to any destination in the world, such Cummins parts to South Africa . Imagine how helpful it is to receive all your spares and technical advice via one supplier. One invoice, fast shipping and a dedicated contact person.
