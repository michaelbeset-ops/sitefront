 Order a Scania genset at Cornerpoint
 Are you looking for a second-hand Scania genset, that you can safely rely on? At Cornerpoint we sell used Scania gensets. In addition, we install and maintain these gensets.

 Why second-hand Scania gensets 
 Many enterprises choose for second-hand, since it saves investing in a new diesel engine. 
 The used Scania genset is always extensively checked and tested. 
 Buying a used genset for backup or emergency requires less paperwork than a new one. 
 When you buy a new genset, you usually need to purchase it as is. With a used genset you have more flexibility to make adjustments.
