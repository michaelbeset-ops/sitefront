 Marine parts supplier
 Cornerpoint is your marine parts supplier. We're based in The Netherlands (Europe) but can ship all items quickly to you anywhere in the world. We've stocked all valuable spares for the marine industry, such as pumps, compressors, contact switches, exhaust grids and invertors. These spares are from different brands, such as Cummins and Scania. All our marine spare parts can be used for maintenance, repair and overhaul. Some of these items are used, but most are brand new. Our stock in constantly changing, please let us know if you're looking for a specific item that is currently not listed on our website. We'll find it for you!
 Global marine parts supplier
 We are an engine specialist for the shipping and offshore industry. We also supply marine equipment, such as dredging machines and tools and have years of experience with different types of engines and generator sets . We work with various International and high-quality brands. Our technicians have worked on a range of different types of ships and vessels and can advise about spare parts for a certain engine or ship. You can order all your ship engine parts and have these items sent to you anywhere in the world, as Cornerpoint is a global marine parts supplier.
