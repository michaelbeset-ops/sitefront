 Worldwide fast delivery
 Once ordered and ready for delivery, Cornerpoint ensures solid and durable packaging of our valued products. Depending on weight, dimensions, destination and desired delivery time, Cornerpoint can offer you several options of shipping. All our years in business we have built strong bonds with several transport companies. Airfreight, sea-freight, consolidation transport, express service delivery or even a special courier are all shipping options. 
 Warehousing
 Cornerpoint has also a consolidation and warehousing service:
 Consolidation: Receive all your marine spare parts via one supplier. One invoice, limited administrative tasks and one technician to advise you on the best option for you. We offer remote and on board service. We send spare parts to anywhere in the world. 
 Warehousing: If you have the same type of engines in your fleet, it is too expensive to store spares on each vessel. Cornerpoint can then store these spares for you and can arrange shipment to the next harbor, if required.
