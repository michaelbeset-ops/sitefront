 URGENT MAK 452AK GENUINE bearings
 One of our clients requested in February a price for genuine bearings of a 6M453AK for budgeting. Quote was made and kept on file for later use…
 Until after a couple of months we received and e-mail overnight that says “ …URGENT”. Then we know enough.
   9:00  Quote updated.
 13:00  Client orders the parts.
 15:00  Parts collected by our courier.
 16:00  Courier arrives back at our stores, parts were checked and numbers verified. Goods were re-packed                  suitable for export and client was consulted to discuss delivery options.
 16:30  Goods dispatched for next days delivery at the shipyard in Poland.
