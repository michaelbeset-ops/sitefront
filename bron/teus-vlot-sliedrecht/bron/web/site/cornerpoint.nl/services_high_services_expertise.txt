 No secrets
 Diesel engines are the beating heart in our customers' vessels as the power sources for propulsion or for driving crucial equipment such as generators and pumps. We know better than anyone else what performance is required from a diesel engine and what it takes to reliably deliver this level of performance.
 Teus Vlot is the most logical choice for the supply and expert fitting, maintenance, repair and overhauling of your diesel engine; especially for Cummins, Scania, Yanmar and SISU Diesel engines, as we are an official licensed dealer for these brands. Another big advantage is that we can also provide everything that is required around the engine. This means that we are specialists in finishing your engine entirely according to your wishes in order to produce a complete, perfectly functioning piece of equipment.
