 Reconditioned ship engine parts
 Cornerpoint is a worldwide supplier of reconditioned ship engine parts. We supply, fit, maintain and overhaul a wide range of diesel engine parts for the shipping industry. Whether you need a used crankshaft or a reconditioned turbocharger, with Cornerpoint your engine revisions are guaranteed to be succesfull. We maintain and repair all these diesel engines in our own workshop. Thanks to our many years of experience, we have extensive knowledge of the most commonly used marine diesel engines and engine parts.
 Advantages of reconditioned ship engine parts 
 Reconditioned ship engine parts are less expensive.  
 New marine engines involve more complicated paperwork. 
 Reconditioned marine engine parts are durable. You reuse the ship’s engine, but we replace worn parts. 
 We extensively test all our overhauled engine parts.

 Complete overhaul of your marine engine 
 We maintain and repair all diesel engines in our own workshop. We can also overhaul your marine engine. We proceed as follows: 
 Our trained technicians will disassemble your diesel engine. Then we clean and check every part. We replace parts that can no longer be used. This way your engine can last for a long time without problems. 
 We also investigate which parts need to be replaced to prevent reactive maintenance in the future. 
 We provide you with a maintenance report of your marine engine.  
 Our maintain and repair activities 
 A small selection of our maintenance and repair work: 
 Check your ship's engine for tension and wear; 
  Change the oil and replace your fuel filter; 
 Inspection of the gearboxes, the cooling water system, the condition of the battery and the quality of the impeller; 
 Replacing the gearbox; 
 Replace/repair fuel pump.
