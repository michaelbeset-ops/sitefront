 Cummins engine (spare) parts  
 Are you looking for Cummins engine (spare) parts? At Cornerpoint we have the knowledge and expertise required to work on Cummins engines: we maintain, repair and overhaul Cummins engines. In addition, we have a large selection of Cummins (engine) spare parts and accessories.   
 Advantages of Cummins engine parts 
 Cummins Inc. is a famous manufacturer for diesel and gas engines, a series of high-class generators and various industrial equipment. Cummins engines power cargo ships, dredging vessels, fishing boats, different types of ferries and many more boats within the operational and commercial marine industry. They have the following advantages, among others:  
 low fuel consumption; 
 low noise; 
 big output power; 
 Durability, which means that reconditioned Cummins spares can be cleaned, checked and used again.

 Activities overhaul Cummins  
 Our overhaul activities can vary from repairing a full Cummins engine to installing Cummins engine spare parts. We proceed as follows: 
 We will disassemble your entire marine engine. 
 We check all Cummins marine engine parts. We replace the parts that are worn out. 
 Thirdly, we clean and assemble your parts. 
 Finally, we hone the machine engine blocks.  
 To ensure maximum lifespan, spare parts need to be replaced by authentic Cummins spares.
