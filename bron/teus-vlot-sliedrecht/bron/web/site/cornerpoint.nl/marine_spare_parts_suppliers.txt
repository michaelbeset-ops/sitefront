 Marine spare parts suppliers
 Cornerpoint is the go-to one-stop-shop amongst marine spare parts suppliers. We sell various kinds of spare parts for the marine industry. We are based in The Netherlands, and ship worldwide. Our range of spares extends from engines and generator sets to small spare parts such as tubes, pressure meters and valves. In our online store you will find many high quality parts for vessels, from brands such as Scania and Caterpillar for ship engines to camshafts and cylinder heads from MAN or Deutz. As one of the top marine spare parts suppliers we are proud of our extensive stock!
 Worldwide marine spare parts supplier
 Many marine spare parts suppliers ship only in certain regions. At Cornerpoint, we serve our customers worldwide. We are a total supplier of ship spare parts and thus specialize in any type of ship spare part, from engines to small parts. Our goal is to buy new and secondhand parts, revise them and then distribute them worldwide. Our mechanics can help you to install all parts where necessary. 
 Specialist in diesel engines for vessels
 We have extensive knowledge of generator sets and diesel engines for vessels. Especially since we are official dealer for a set of powerful and well-known brands; Cummins, Scania, Yanmar and SISU. We sell, buy and overhaul these high-end ship engines so that they can have a second life. In this way we can offer clients good value for money, while at the same time supporting a circular economy.

 Cornerpoint: part of Teus Vlot Diesel & Marine
 We originated as a diesel engine repair company, which is known as Teus Vlot. Teus Vlot has been involved in the maintenance and assembly of marine diesel engines since 1992. The special thing about our company is that we offer all parts under one roof: in addition to diesel technology, we offer electrical engineering, machining and overhaul technology. How do we really distinguish ourselves? As a skipper you always have only one point of contact with us. If you place your projects with us, this will save you considerably in time.
