 Cornerpoint
 Cornerpoint is the worldwide supplier of a wide range of diesel engines, spare parts and related equipment. Our logistic services deliver you the correct parts, professionally packed, on the first available flight to any destination in the world.

 Why Cornerpoint?
 Cornerpoint organises the purchase and sale of parts for ships and repair companies. Selling could be anybody's business; expertise and decent service is ours! And we go even further; new horizons and the high seas are our daily business. Cornerpoint is part of the Teus Vlot group . Therefore, we can rely on the technical knowledge of 75 people.
