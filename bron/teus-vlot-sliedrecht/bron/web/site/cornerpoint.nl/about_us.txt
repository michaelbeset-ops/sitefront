 The company
 A reputable name for one-stop-shopping in the world of shipping. Cornerpoint organises the purchase and sale of parts for ships and repair companies. Selling could be anybody’s business, expertise and decent service is ours. And Cornerpoint goes even further; new horizons and the high seas are our daily business. Cornerpoint is part of  Teus Vlot Diesel & Marine . This service minded company is originally started as a diesel engine repair company. During many years the company has grown in its activities and capabilities.
