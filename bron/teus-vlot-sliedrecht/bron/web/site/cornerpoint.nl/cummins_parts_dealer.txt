 Official Cummins parts dealer
 Cornerpoint is official Cummins parts dealer and distributes spare parts worldwide. Order your products online, or call to discuss what item needs to be replaced. You can expect professional packaging, secure transport and a quick delivery. 
 Order your new or revised Cummins spares online
 As an original Cummins distributor and dealer, you can order all parts directly with us. Our office is located in Sliedrecht, the Netherlands. Check out our range of genuine Cummins parts, both revised and new spares and order them online.  Your items will be wrapped and sent safely via airmail as quick as possible. Can't find a specific replacement part? Let us know . We will get our hands on this item, only adding one or two days to its total shipping time. 
 Genuine versus non-genuine Cummins parts
 It's advisable to replace a part of a Cummins engine or generator with a genuine, original Cummins spare part. Cummins parts are of the highest quality, designed to perform and last. Cummins engines have proven to last a very long time. Even the seemingly smallest part can make a critical difference in a certain operation or mechanical function. The difference between genuine and non-genuine Cummins spares are almost never clearly visible. Using different parts may seem like a cheaper option, but could lead to problems and therefore budget waste in the future. To prevent wear, any problems or failure - it is best to replace items with original Cummins parts. Buy Cummins parts online with us.
