 Cummins power generation
 A Cummins power generation system consists of different components, installations and technologies to supply power to buildings and means of transport. For instance, hospitals and schools. Or for boats and offshore vessels such as cargo ships and dredging vessels. Cummins power generation sets (gensets) can form a primary source of power, be used for separate critical operations or installed for emergency backup power.
 Our service around Cummins power generation
 Cummins stand for proven technology, reliability and durability. Cummins power generation sets are based on powerful design to propagate these values. There are three types of Cummins power generation sets, namely: gas, diesel and marine. They all require regular maintenance and can only be serviced by knowledgeable technicians. We have been installing, maintaining and repairing pars of Cummins power generation sets for many years.
