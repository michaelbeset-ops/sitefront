 Cornerpoint for marine parts 
 Find various new, used and reconditioned marine (spare) parts, replaceable items and specific ship accessories. Cornerpoint is your source for marine parts. With a background in maritime logistics, we know what your engine, generator, ship or offshore vessel needs. When it comes to repairing or replacing parts, we've got all the required spares for you. 
 Our marine parts in stock 
 We sell items from different brands such as: 
 Deutz 
 MWM 
 Cummins 
 Amot 
 Scania 
 Bosch  
 We have a wide range of spares, containing branded and separate spare pars in stock, for example: gensets or engine blocks, couplings, camshafts and small valve cages.  These spares are new, used and reconditioned. More items are added to our inventory every day. We can also find different items on request. 
 Not sure what item you need? Let us know , our team can assist you in finding the right spare part. 
 New, used or reconditioned marine parts 
 Whether buying new, used or reconditioned marine spare parts - we've checked, cleaned, tested or inspected them fully. The difference between used and reconditioned marine parts is that a reconditioned engine is equipped with new parts. That is not the case with an used one, although it has been extensively tested and verified. 
 Why should you buy used or reconditioned marine parts? A few advantages: 
 New marine parts are more expensive.  
 Used and refurbished ship parts are very suitable for use as a spare or back-up.
