 (Spare) engine parts of a ship 
 The engine parts of a ship are continuously subject to small damages caused by intensive or even normal use. These wear parts can be parts such as generator sets, reverse gearboxes, pumps, filters and other engine parts. At Cornerpoint, we overhaul all ship diesel engines and we provide (spare) engine parts for different types of ships such as coasters, floating dredgers and barges.

 Activities complete ship engine overhaul 
 Do you need engine parts, but also lack the technical knowledge to overhaul your engine yourself? We can do a complete overhaul of the ship engine. We do the following work: 
 Our specialized mechanics take out all the engine parts of your ship that need to be inspected.  
 We check all the parts and overhaul the turbo, water pumps, injector nozzles, and other engine components.  
 On the parts we do ultrasonic cleaning, fitting of valves and valve seats, engine blocks, and repairing and overhauling of connecting rods. 
 Additionally, we drill, bore, hone and machine engine blocks.
