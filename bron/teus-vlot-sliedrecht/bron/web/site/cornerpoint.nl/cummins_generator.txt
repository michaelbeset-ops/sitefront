 Used or new Cummins generator 
 Are you looking into buying a new Cummins generator? Or are you interested in options of used Cummins generators? We have new as well as used Cummins industrial, diesel and gas generators (also known as: gensets).  
 All our Cummins machinery and spare parts are authentic, checked, 100% reliable and still in great shape. We can offer you a used Cummins generator at a fair price. Moreover, we will take care of a quick delivery, to any location in the world. You can also count on us in providing the best quality service. What else do you need? 
 What are the benefits of buying a used Cummins generator? 
 Investing in Cummins means making a solid investment. Buying a used Cummins generator instead of a new one will save you a lot of money. 
 Used Cummins Industrial Generators work like new and have been extensively tested. 
 Companies usually go through various formalities and paperwork when buying brand new Cummins generators. There will still be a few papers to complete to complete the sale, but they will be easier to handle

 How to buy a used Cummins generator? 
 There are a couple of things that you need to take into account before buying a used Cummins generator: 
 Firstly, you'll want to keep a clear budget and any specific requests in mind.  
 You will also need to compare prices between used and new models and you can ask to see several Cummins generators being tested.  
 Here at Cornerpoint, we can tell you all you need to know about the history of a certain Cummins genset. For instance, where and for what purpose it was stationed somewhere. 
 We will also inform you about its manufacturing date, its working hours, its current state in details and its history in maintenance.  
 Please share your requirements with our technicians, they can advise you on several options. We'll help you find the best new or used Cummins generator for you.
