 Another Stamford alternator 475kVA this time
 The rumour of the quick delivery of the 1800kVA alternator went through the market and brought another enquiry up. 475kVA this time. There were 4 pcs Stamford HCM534 E2 available and we could reserve 1 for our client until later notice. After a couple of days, client confirmed the order. This alternator was type approved and not yet classed. Luckily we managed to get the alternator tested incl BV surveyor in 3 days time and soon after this alternator flew out from AMS airport.
