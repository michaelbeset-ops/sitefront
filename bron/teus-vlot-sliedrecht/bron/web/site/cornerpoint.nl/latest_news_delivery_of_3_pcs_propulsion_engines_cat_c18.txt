 Delivery of 3 pcs propulsion engines CAT C18
 Cornerpoint B.V. delivered this week brand new Caterpillar C18 marine propulsion engines.
 These engines including control systems and spare parts will send directly to the end user for repowering there vessel. If you are in need of diesel engines, gearboxes or spare parts, you can contact Cornerpoint for world wide supply of your needs.
 Cornerpoint is your supplier for a wide range of new and used motors, motor parts, couplings, generator sets and other components. We work with brands such as Cummins, Scania, MAN, Deutz and Wartsila, anywhere in the world.
 Cornerpoint is part of the Teus Vlot Group. That means that we work together with their specialists in engine's, electrical engineering and ironwork for the maritime shipping and inland navigation sectors, as well as industry. The Teus Vlot Group is an official dealer of Cummins, Scania and Agco Power products.
