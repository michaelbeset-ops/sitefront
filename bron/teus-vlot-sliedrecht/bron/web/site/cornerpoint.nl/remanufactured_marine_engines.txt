 Industrial and marine engines
 Is it hard to find the engine you are looking for? Cornerpoint arranges the purchase and sale of both industrial and marine engines. We are able to remanufacture and install a wide variety of engines in our own harbor in Sliedrecht. This enables us to provide you with tailored solutions and the best possible service. 
 What engines can you find at Cornerpoint?
 Whether you are looking for an industrial or a marine engine, Cornerpoint is committed to find the product you are looking for. We carry many industrial and marine engine brands, such as Daf, Volvo and Scania. Many of which are directly available from our stock, ready to be shipped to any destination in the world. 
 New, used or remanufactured?
 The great amount of possible solutions we offer at Cornerpoint may make it more difficult to choose. How do you decide whether you need a new of remanufactured marine engine for example? Should you need any advice on these kind of questions, please do not hesitate to contact us. We are happy to help!
